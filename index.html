Before you book

Studio policies

Deposit to book

A deposit is required to secure your appointment. The deposit is applied to your final price, and the amount is shown at checkout. PLACEHOLDER: deposit terms and refund rules.

18+ only, valid ID

You must be 18 or older. Every client must show a valid government photo ID at the appointment. PLACEHOLDER: minor and consent policy.

Free consultation

Consultations are free. We discuss subject, size, placement and detail before we quote your piece. PLACEHOLDER: consult booking method.

Large pieces and same-day

Large pieces are not done same-day. They are planned with a consult and booked for a later session. PLACEHOLDER: lead time for large work.

Walk-ins

Walk-ins are welcome for small pieces and piercings when an artist has a gap. Booked appointments come first. PLACEHOLDER: walk-in hours.

Aftercare

Keep the wrap on for the time your artist gives you. Wash gently with unscented soap, pat dry, apply a thin layer of the recommended balm, and avoid sun, swimming and picking for at least two weeks. You will get written aftercare instructions after each session.
